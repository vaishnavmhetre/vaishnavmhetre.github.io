I am a platform engineer with approximately seven years of experience working primarily in **Java**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js** on **GCP** with **Spring Boot**.

My career spans two domains that shaped how I think about systems at scale — and one common thread running through both of them.

## Experience

### NielsenIQ — market data

I work on **binary data delivery at scale**: querying retail measurement data out of databases and shipping it to downstream systems as **binary payloads** through the delivery pipeline's filters and enrichments, rather than as documents. I have contributed to the pipeline's configuration-handling framework and to its file-handling service, and I supervised the migration of prediction services (such as a pricing forecast backend) onto the delivery pipeline — making them robust. I now work on AI-driven initiatives.

### Allianz Technology — motor insurance

I worked on a motor-insurance platform, operating at the intersection of Contract, Claims, and Document lifecycles. I led a five-person document team and drove the shift toward event-driven systems and microservices.

Across both roles, the common thread is **data delivery** — taking data out of databases and getting it, reliably and at scale, into the form downstream consumers actually need: binary payloads today, documents earlier in my career.
