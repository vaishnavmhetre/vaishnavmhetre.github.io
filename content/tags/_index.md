The tag vocabulary is deliberately small: five terms, chosen because each one
describes a thread that actually runs through the work rather than a passing
detail. `data-delivery` and `platform` each span more than one entry, so they
are the tags worth browsing. The remaining terms are carried by a single entry
and are listed here, but their own pages are not indexed — a one-entry page is
a thin page, and thin pages are not worth a search result.
