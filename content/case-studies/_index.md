title: "Case Studies"
description: "How problems from real platform work actually got solved: binary data delivery for retail measurement, migrating prediction services onto a shared pipeline, and the foundations AI initiatives depend on."
---

Each case study is a problem I worked on, written up as the frame, the approach,
and the trade-offs. None of them is a product pitch — the point is the
reasoning, including the parts that turned out to be expensive.

The same threads run through all of them: getting data out of databases and into
the shape downstream consumers need, moving services onto shared infrastructure
instead of letting each one build its own path, and keeping that infrastructure
dependable enough for AI initiatives to be built on top of it.
